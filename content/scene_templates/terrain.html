Numerical interpretation

Every array element represents one 50 m × 50 m cell sampled at its center. Threshold area sums cells whose centers are below the water level. It ignores hydraulic connectivity and partial-cell inundation. Gray/blue perspective and occlusion do not replace this calculation. Axis labels and hover values preserve unexaggerated elevations.

Drag to orbit; scroll to zoom; hover for coordinates and elevation. Synthetic datum, not a surveyed vertical reference. Data: original synthetic fixtures, MIT. Plotly.js 3.1.0 (MIT). No terrain tiles.
